Financial Analysis Question Generator (Production Ready – Final)
You are generating advanced financial analysis questions to test model-breaking reasoning on corporate financial reports (PDFs).
Each question must simulate real-world financial decision-making scenarios, combining multi-table and visual reasoning with derived financial metrics.
---
:link: Core Rules
:x: Do NOT provide answers, hints, formulas, page references, or chart/table titles.
:x: Do NOT include hints inside the question such as “look at…”, “from the chart…”, or “check the footnotes…”.
✅ The question must be straight and self-contained, without guiding the solver where or how to look.
Every question must satisfy ALL the following:
1. Visual Usage
   * Each question must use ≥ 2 visuals:
     - Tabular financial visuals: Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, KPI table, Footnotes
     - Optional: Flowchart, Waterfall Chart, or Process Diagram for specific categories
   * Indirect visual references only:
     - ✅ “lowest orange bar in the CapEx bridge”
     - ✅ “thin blue arrow in the free cash flow diagram”
     - ❌ “Table 3” or “Page 5”
   * ❌ Do NOT give hints about where to look or what to extract.
2. Advanced Financial Logic
   * Must involve derived calculations, such as:
     - ROE, ROIC, FCFE, FCFF, CapEx Intensity, Working Capital Turnover, EBITDA margin chain
   * Multi-step chain reasoning:
     - Medium: ≥ 25 steps
     - Hard: ≥ 40 steps
   * Must combine ≥ 2 report sections.
3. Multi-Assumption & Scenario Logic
   * Exactly 3 questions must include multi-assumption what-if scenarios, e.g.:
     - “if interest expense increases by 20% and dividend payout ratio decreases to 30%…”
   * Scenario must affect a derived metric, not a raw line item.
4. Flowchart / Diagram Integration
   * Exactly 3 questions must involve flowcharts, waterfall charts, or process diagrams.
   * Must require numeric computation from these visuals, but ❌ do not tell the user where to look.
   * ✅ Use indirect visual language: “dotted segment in the operational flow,” “tallest blue bar.”
5. Hard Numeric Chain Questions
   * Exactly 4 questions must be hard numeric multi-table derivations:
     - ≥ 40-step reasoning, derived ratios, multi-year comparisons
     - No flowchart or scenario required
6. Single, Objective Output
   * Each question yields one number or short finance phrase (e.g., ROE %, net debt-to-EBITDA multiple).
   * ❌ No subjective “why” or “evaluate” phrasing.
   * ✅ No hints or directions included in the question itself.
7. Language Quality
   * One grammatically correct sentence ≥ 22 words per question.
   * Must simulate an equity analyst’s inquiry and avoid vague or guiding language.
