# Prompt: Generate a Detailed, Visual-Centric, Investor-Oriented Summary of a Corporate Financial Report (REV 3 – PDF Summary)
You are a **visual document analyst** reviewing selected pages from a **corporate annual or quarterly financial report**. Your task is to generate a **detailed, investor-focused summary** that is:
* grounded in **visual content** (e.g., tables, charts, infographics),
* rich with **financial relevance** for analysts,
* designed to surface **model-breaking QA opportunities**.
This summary will help annotators understand the full layout and analytical value of the report for LLM evaluation.
---
## ✅ Output Format
Each section should contain **200–400 words**, ensuring depth and completeness while remaining concise enough for annotator consumption.
Use **stars (`*`) as bullets**. Your summary must be broken into the following five required sections:
### 1. **Major Report Sections**
* List high-level sections such as: MD\&A, Chairman/CEO Letters, Segment Highlights, Financial Highlights, ESG, Risk Factors, Consolidated Financial Statements, and Notes.
* Include notes if a section is **spread across multiple pages** or contains rich visuals that span years, segments, or geographies.
### 2. **Key Financial Highlights (Investor POV)**
* Highlight major reported KPIs: Revenue, EBITDA, Net Earnings, CapEx, Operating Cash Flow, Net Debt, Working Capital.
* Where possible, **include YoY % change**, CAGR, or margin insight (e.g., EBITDA margin trend, gross margin expansion).
* Prioritize metrics that impact valuation (e.g., ROE, ROIC, FCFE, EPS, debt leverage ratios).
### 3. **Strategic and Operational Insights**
* Summarize any growth strategies, restructuring, geographic expansion, acquisitions, CapEx ramp-up, margin recovery, technology deployments, or sustainability initiatives.
* Indicate if there are visuals directly tied to these initiatives (e.g., regional expansion maps, asset deployment tables).
### 4. **Detailed Visual Content Index**
* Provide a **page-by-page or section-based rundown of charts, tables, and infographics**.
* For each visual, describe:
  * Type (bar chart, pie chart, line graph, table, waterfall, map)
  * Content (what metric or breakdown it shows)
  * Time span (multi-year or single year)
  * Segmentation (region, segment, product, cost center, etc.)
  * Notable layout features (e.g., stacked, colored by geography, vertical bridge, dual-axis)
Examples:
* Bar chart: 5-year revenue by region (stacked by segment)
* Waterfall chart: EBITDA to net earnings reconciliation with labeled adjustments (2022)
* Pie chart: FY 2022 revenue mix by customer type
* Table: Segment-wise CapEx and operating income side-by-side for 3 years
* Map: Global operational footprint with flags for active rigs and acquisitions
### 5. **Model-Breaking QA Signal (Promptability)**
* Call out visuals that enable:
  * **Multi-step calculations** (CAGR, YoY %, margin shifts)
  * **Cross-visual metric synthesis** (e.g., CapEx from one chart + cash from another → FCFE)
  * **Scenario math** (e.g., what-if CapEx dropped by 10%)
  * **Complex ratio derivation** (ROE, interest coverage, net-debt-to-EBITDA)
* Flag visuals with at least two of the following:
  * Multi-year time series
  * Segment-level granularity
  * Embedded subtotals requiring aggregation
  * Distinction between GAAP and adjusted metrics
---
## 🔒 Style Rules
* Use **clear, bullet-style markdown** with `*` at the start of each line.
* Emphasize **investor relevance** — what would a financial analyst care about?
* Be **visually descriptive** — don’t just say "table of revenue", say “4-row table showing 3-year revenue by product type with YoY % column”.
* Avoid copying narrative unless it’s visually paired or critical context.
* Never hallucinate a chart or table — only describe what's visibly present.

  * Product Pipeline Overview: Visual table showing asset progress from discovery through commercialization (page 8)

  * Chairman’s Letter: Strategic overview, no visuals (page 15)
  * Licensing & Acquisition Agreements: Financial milestones and partner breakdowns with strategic commentary (pages 16–19)
* **Key Financial Highlights**
  * \$80M upfront payment from Genentech (page 16)
  * Up to \$485M total potential milestone payments noted in Genentech agreement
  * Huadong deal structure includes upfront plus \$45M+ in contingent milestones (page 18)
  * Implied R\&D intensity visible via clinical pipeline and trial design visuals, though no direct P\&L or expense table shown
* **Strategic and Operational Insights**
  * ARCALYST positioning as anchor asset in U.S. commercialization efforts
  * Focus on immunology pipeline with late-stage biologics in KPL-404 development
  * China and Asia expansion via Huadong partnership; signals non-U.S. monetization intent
  * Licensing strategy enables cash inflow without equity dilution — critical in pre-revenue biotech model
* **Detailed Visual Content Index**
  * Page 8: Visual pipeline chart (table layout) showing molecule-wise progress: Discovery, IND-enabling, Phases 1–3, Commercial
  * Page 13:
    * Graph 1: KPL-404 receptor occupancy vs. dose — curved line with 4 dosing arms
    * Graph 2: Serum concentration vs. time — PK profile over 7 days
  * Pages 16–19: Contract language interspersed with financial disclosures; includes figures like \$80M upfront, \$485M max value, \$45M milestone; structured paragraph-style, not tabular
* **Model-Breaking QA Signal**
  * Multi-part licensing agreement values → enable total valuation math, scenario variation (e.g., upfront as % of max payout)
  * Clinical trial visual data → supports prompts on slope analysis, dose-response implications, pharmacokinetics
  * Product pipeline visual → supports segment aggregation (e.g., number of Phase 2 assets), what-if promotion scenarios, stage-weighted portfolio prompts
  * Indirect milestone references require numerical extraction from embedded text → forces visual + semantic synthesis
* **Major Sections**
  * Chairman's Letter (1 page)
  * MD\&A: spans 6 pages, includes segment performance tables and margin walk bridges
  * Financial Highlights section with bolded year-over-year comparatives
  * Consolidated Financial Statements and Notes (final third of report)
* **Key Financial Highlights**
  * Revenue: \$2.3B, up 8% YoY; EBITDA margin improved from 21.4% to 23.1%
  * CapEx up 18% YoY, largely for fleet modernization
  * Free Cash Flow positive after 3 years negative; ROIC now at 11.8%
* **Strategic and Operational Insights**
  * Acquisition of regional drilling firm (noted in timeline visual)
  * Cost optimization program reduced SG\&A by 12%
  * R\&D chart shows increased investment in automation tech
* **Detailed Visual Content Index**
  * Stacked bar chart: 5-year revenue by product and geography (page 5)
  * Dual-axis line + bar: EBITDA vs CapEx, quarterly view (page 7)
  * Table: 3-year segment-level profitability vs CapEx (page 6)
  * Map: Global operations, color-coded by rig count (page 4)
* **Model-Breaking QA Signal**
  * EBITDA margin bridge → enables %‑point delta prompts
  * CapEx + cash flow → supports FCFE / FCF yield calculations
  * Pie + table revenue split → usable for scenario-based revenue prompts
  * Multi-year earnings + equity → supports 3-year ROE trend prompt
