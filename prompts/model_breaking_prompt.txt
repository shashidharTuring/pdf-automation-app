Financial Analysis Question Generator (Production Ready – Final)
You are generating advanced financial analysis questions to test model-breaking reasoning on corporate financial reports.  
Each question must simulate real-world financial decision-making scenarios, combining multi-table and visual reasoning with derived financial metrics.
---
:link: Core Rules
:x: Do NOT provide answers, hints, formulas, page references, or chart/table titles.  
Every question must satisfy ALL the following:  
1. Visual Usage  
   * Each question must use ≥ 2 visuals:  
     - Tabular financial visual (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, KPI table, Footnotes)  
     - Optional diagram/flowchart visual for specific question categories  
   * Indirect visual references only:  
     - ✅ “lowest orange bar in the CapEx bridge”  
     - ✅ “thin blue arrow in the free cash flow diagram”  
     - ❌ “Table 3” or “Page 5”  
2. Advanced Financial Logic  
   * Must involve derived calculations:  
     - ROE, ROIC, FCFE, FCFF, CapEx Intensity, Working Capital Turnover, EBITDA margin chain  
   * Multi-step chain reasoning:  
     - Medium = 25+ steps  
     - Hard = 40+ steps  
   * Combine ≥ 2 concepts from different report sections.
3. Multi-Assumption & Scenario Logic  
   * Exactly 3 questions must include multi-assumption what-if scenarios:  
     - e.g., “if interest expense increases by 20% and dividend payout ratio decreases to 30%…”  
   * Scenario must affect a derived metric, not a raw line item.
4. Flowchart/Diagram Integration  
   * Exactly 3 questions must involve flowcharts, waterfall charts, or process diagrams  
     - Must require extracting numeric info from these visuals to calculate a ratio or derived metric.  
   * Indirect visual language (e.g., “dotted segment in the operational flow”).
5. Hard Numeric Chain Questions  
   * Exactly 4 questions must be hard numeric questions:  
     - 40+ step reasoning, multi-table chain, no flowchart or scenario required.
6. Single, Objective Output  
   * Each question yields a single number or short finance phrase (e.g., ROE %, net debt-to-EBITDA multiple).  
   * ❌ No subjective “why” or “evaluate” phrasing.
7. Language Quality  
   * One grammatically correct sentence ≥ 22 words per question.  
   * Must simulate an equity analyst’s inquiry and avoid vague language.
