# Prompt: Craft 10 Quantitative, Model-Breaking Finance Questions

You are an **annotator** reviewing a **Corporate Annual Report** (PDF).
Generate **ten** questions that will thoroughly test a large‑language model’s finance and numerical‑reasoning skills.

---

## What to return (exactly)

For **each** of the ten questions output **two bullets**:

1. **Question** – *one sentence, at least 20 words, whose answer is a single number (currency, ratio, percentage, count) or one short finance phrase*.
2. **Why it’s challenging** – *one or two short sentences* describing the multi‑step reasoning or finance nuance that makes the question hard.

No answers, hints, or extra commentary—just those two bullets per question.

---

## Hard rules you **must** follow

| Theme                       | Requirements                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Visual grounding**        | • Each question must be answerable **only by examining at least one chart, table, diagram, or infographic** in the report.<br>• Refer to visuals **indirectly** (e.g., “the tallest blue bar in the operating‑profit waterfall”)—never by page number, chart title, or file name.                            |                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Single objective answer** | • The correct response must be **one number** (currency, ratio, percentage, count) **or one short phrase** (e.g., “free‑cash‑flow margin”).<br>• The question **must require at least one arithmetic or logical operation** (sum, difference, ratio, YoY delta, weighted average, what‑if adjustment, etc.). |                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Finance SME scope**       | • Use authentic corporate‑finance concepts: EBITDA margin, ROIC, working‑capital days, debt‑to‑equity, CapEx, segment profitability, stress‑test coverage, etc.<br>• Across the set, cover **at least three distinct finance topics**.                                                                       |                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Reasoning complexity**    | • Demand **multi‑step reasoning**—cross‑visual look‑ups, aggregation across rows, trend comparison, scenario math, indirect deduction.<br>• Vary reasoning styles (percentage‑point change, CAGR, composite metric derivation, multi‑segment consolidation).                                                 |                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Difficulty mix**          | • Provide **4 hard** and **6 hard** questions:<br> – *hard* ≈ 10‑15 reasoning steps.<br> – *Hard* ≈ 15‑25 steps or multi‑visual, multi‑year synthesis.                                                                                                                                                   | 
| **Language quality**        | • Grammatically correct, precise, and realistic—something an equity analyst might genuinely ask.<br>• Avoid subjective asks (“Why did…”, “How effective…”) and all names/PII.                                                                                                                                |                                                                                                                                                                                                |
