**Refined Instruction Prompt for Generating Complex Financial Analysis Questions**

You are generating financial analysis tasks designed for evaluating large language models (LLMs). Each task must simulate real-world analytical questions grounded in authentic financial reporting logic. Your prompts will require financial SME-level understanding and should reflect scenarios relevant to investment analysis, corporate finance, or operational decision-making.

---

## 🔗 Core Requirements

**Mandatory Criteria for Every Prompt**:

1. **Use at least two visual elements** (tables, charts, graphs, or footnotes) from financial reports.

   * Acceptable sources: Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement, Footnotes, MD\&A, Notes, Stock Price Table, or KPIs table.
   * Do not use decorative visuals or headers.

2. **Involve advanced reasoning and finance SME logic.**

   * Must use financial ratios or chain computations (e.g., ROE, EBITDA Margin, WC Turnover, CapEx Intensity).
   * Must require 25+ logical steps for medium questions, and 40+ for hard questions.
   * Must require combining concepts from multiple sections of the report.

3. **Cross-reference across multiple tables and/or textual narratives.**

   * You MUST include at least one computation that uses data from **two or more distinct tables or sections** (e.g., use of inventory data from notes and revenue from IS to compute turnover).

4. **Include at least two "what-if" or hypothetical assumptions** in the overall prompt set.

   * These must affect ratios or derived metrics, not just single raw line items.
   * Each assumption should be financially realistic and tied to stakeholder decisions (e.g., "if interest expense increased by 20% due to refinancing").

5. **NO unnecessary or generic instructions.**

   * Avoid phrasing like "assume all else remains the same" unless it prevents ambiguity.
   * Do NOT refer to page numbers or chart names directly. Use indirect references like "bottom-most bar", "blue line", "dotted segment", etc.

6. **Must be solvable with a single correct and objective answer.**

---

## 📅 Task Set Requirements

* Total Prompts: **5**

  * **3 Hard** (40+ steps, multi-table, ratio-driven, visually complex)
  * **2 Medium** (25+ steps, at least two visuals, chain logic)
  * Include **2 prompts with multi-assumption what-if logic**

---

## 🔄 Output Format Per Prompt:

### ✅ Prompt {n} (**{Difficulty}**; **Visuals:** {brief visual source combo})

**Prompt:**
Write a clear, real-world financial question that requires the model to reason over multiple visuals, include SME logic, and reflect business decision contexts. The prompt should require 25–40+ reasoning steps and may involve computing, comparing, cross-referencing, and/or adjusting financial metrics.

**Rationale:**

* Describe **what financial concept** or ratio is tested
* Explain **what tables/sections** are used (e.g., Income Statement + Footnote + Cash Flow table)
* List **reasoning types involved** (e.g., ratio chain, multi-table comparison, what-if scenario)

---

## ✅ Additional Constraint:

Each prompt must include **at least one sentence of 25+ words** in the problem statement, ensuring natural, purposeful framing without sacrificing analytical complexity.
